## Full description (4,000 characters max)

```
EM Sim is a simulation suite for emergency medicine teaching. A facilitator runs the patient from one device while learners watch a realistic patient monitor, defibrillator or ventilator on other phones, tablets or a TV.

For facilitators:
• Start a Quick Sim, pick one of more than 250 built-in emergency scenarios, or build your own.
• Change heart rate, blood pressure, oxygen saturation, breathing and rhythm live, or set trends that develop over time.
• Give drugs and interventions and see their effects on the patient.
• Show investigations such as blood gases, ECGs, X-rays and ultrasound when the team asks for them.
• Run cardiac arrests with shockable and non-shockable rhythms, defibrillation, pacing and return of circulation.
• Finish with a debrief: a timeline of what was done and when, and the scenario's learning objectives.

For learners:
• A patient monitor with moving waveforms and alarms on a second screen.
• A defibrillator screen for a separate tablet.
• A ventilator screen for airway and critical care scenarios.

Linking screens is simple: each session has a short code and a QR code, so other devices join by scanning or typing it.

An optional free account lets you save your own scenarios and use them on any device. Course scenarios for approved instructors are available after signing in and requesting access.

EM Sim is for simulation training only. It is not a medical device and must not be used for real patient care. Please do not enter real patient information.

Published by West Midlands Evidence Based Emergency Medicine Ltd (EM Evidence).
```

Before pasting, check each line still matches the live site (the scenario count is in `data/scenarios.js`).
