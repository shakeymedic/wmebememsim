Quick Start Guide

EM Evidence Sim · for instructors running scenarios · updated 1 October 2026

What you need

You can run a simulation with one laptop and one screen the team can see. Everything runs in a web browser at wmebemsim.netlify.app; there is nothing to install.

DeviceWho uses itNeeded?
Laptop or tablet: the instructor screenYou, out of the team's sightAlways
A second screen, TV, projector or device: the room monitorThe teamAlways
A tablet: the defibThe teamOnly for Defib Sim, or if you want the team to use a defibrillator
  • Every device needs an internet connection. The screens link to each other online using a session code.
  • Sound (alarms and beeps) plays on the room monitor by default, so turn its volume up.
  • You do not need to sign in to run the built-in scenarios.

Step 1: Open the app

  1. On your laptop, open wmebemsim.netlify.app.
  2. Note the six-character Session Code (for example K7PQ3M). It is shown on the setup screen and in the top right corner as "Session ID".
  3. The code is what links the room monitor and the defib to your screen. Each group can keep the same code all day.

Starting with a new group? Press New code. Any monitor or defib still on the old code will need to join again.

Step 2: Choose a scenario

Pick a tab on the setup screen. For most teaching, use Premade.

TabUse it forHow to start
PremadeA ready-written scenario (anaphylaxis, sepsis, trauma, paediatrics and more)Pick a category, press Load, read the brief, press Start Scenario
Quick SimA blank patient you control by hand, for ad-hoc bedside teachingSet age, weight and starting rhythm, press Start Quick Sim
Defib SimDefibrillation, cardioversion and pacing skills on the defib tabletPick Education or Assessment, pick a scenario, press Start Defib Sim
RandomA generated patient from a category and acuity you choosePress Generate Scenario, then start it
Custom / BuilderScenarios you have written or importedLoad or build one, then start it

The brief shown before Start Scenario has the patient story, equipment, expected progression, key interventions and learning objectives. Read it before the team comes in.

Step 3: Set up the room monitor (and defib)

Room monitor on a second screen plugged into your laptop: press Launch Monitor on the setup screen, or Screens → Launch room monitor once the scenario is running. It opens in a new window; drag it onto the second screen and make it full screen.

Room monitor or defib on a separate device: press Screens → Join by QR code and scan the code with that device's camera. No camera? For the monitor, open the site on the device, press Join Another Session, type the code and press Connect. For the defib, open wmebemsim.netlify.app/defib/ and type the code.

Then:

  1. On the room monitor, tap Tap to Enable Sound. Browsers block sound until someone taps the screen.
  2. Check the badge at the top of your instructor screen. It should read Live and name the linked screen (for example "Live · patient monitor").

Step 4: Run the scenario

You play the patient. When the team does something, you press the matching button, and the monitor and patient respond.

When the team…You press…
Puts on ECG, sats, cuff and temperature probeAttach on the Monitoring & access bar. Until then the room monitor shows no numbers
Asks for a blood pressureCycle NIBP Now. The room monitor only shows a new BP when the cuff cycles. When you change the BP, choose Change, don't send (they see it at the next cycle) or Change and cycle cuff now
Gives a drug or does a procedureFind it in the search box or the tabs (Common, Drugs, Airway…) and tap it
Does something that should change a vital signTap that vital's tile, type the new value, choose Now, 30 s, 2 min or 5 min, then Confirm (BP starts on Now and has its own two buttons)
Lets the patient arrestARREST ▾ and choose the rhythm
Gets the patient backROSC ▾

Also useful:

  • PAUSE / START (or the space bar) stops and starts the clock.
  • Patient condition shows whether the patient gets worse on their own (AUTO) or only when you change things (MANUAL). Better and Worse nudge the whole patient.
  • Event log (bottom right) records everything with a time. Type a note and press Add note, or tap the flag beside any entry to discuss it later.
  • Simple / Full (top bar): Simple hides the advanced controls. Use Full when you need them.

Step 5: Finish and debrief

  1. Press Finish and confirm. The debrief screen opens on your laptop.
  2. Use it to lead the debrief:
    • Performance Summary: which learning objectives were met, partly done or not done.
    • Vitals Trend & Interventions: a graph of the obs with each treatment marked.
    • Flagged events: the log entries you flagged.
    • Session Replay: drag the slider to show the obs at any point in the scenario.
    • Simulation Log: every action with its time.
  3. Press Download Report to keep a copy, or Print. Certificate (Defib Sim only) makes a certificate of completion for the learner.
  4. Press Exit to Menu to set up the next scenario. The room monitor and defib stay linked.

Quick fixes

ProblemFix
Room monitor shows no numbersThe team has not "attached" monitoring yet. Press Attach on the Monitoring & access bar
No soundTap Tap to Enable Sound on the room monitor and check its volume. Tools → Sound plays on chooses which screen plays it
Badge says Sync errorCheck the internet connection on each device
Room monitor or defib not following your changesCheck it has the same session code as your laptop; join again if not
Instructor tab closed by mistakeReopen the site and press Resume under "Resume Previous?"
Defib Sim log times stuck at 00:00The clock has not started. Press START, or it starts at the learner's first press on the defib

Keyboard shortcuts: Space = pause or start; F = finish; D = drug calculator; T = timer alerts; ? = list of shortcuts. If a shortcut does nothing, click an empty part of the screen first.

For everything else, see the Full Instructor Guide.
